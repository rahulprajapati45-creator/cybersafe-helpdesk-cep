🛡️ About Cyber Safety Helpdesk

Cyber Safety Helpdesk helps users understand and respond to common cyber-safety problems.

Users can select their problem such as Fake Account, Suspicious Link, Online Fraud or Hacked Account and receive basic safety guidance.

🔐 Basic Cyber Safety Tips

🔑 Strong Password

Use strong and unique passwords. Never share passwords.

📲 OTP Safety

Never share OTP, PIN or CVV with unknown people.

🔗 Check Links

Do not open unknown or suspicious links.

💳 Payment Safety

Verify the receiver before making online payments.

COMMUNITY CYBER AWARENESS PROGRAM

🛡️ Cyber Help Desk Day

Organize a Cyber Help Desk Day where community members can bring their general cyber-safety questions. Students demonstrate the website and provide awareness guidance.

👥 Community Members

Community members can bring their general cyber-safety questions and discuss common online safety concerns.

💻 Student Demonstration

Students demonstrate the Cyber Safety Helpdesk website and explain how users can use its safety features.

📢 Awareness Guidance

Students provide awareness guidance about fake accounts, suspicious links, online fraud, hacked accounts, OTP safety and secure online practices.

📋 Cyber Help Desk Day Activities

  • Answer general cyber-safety questions.
  • Demonstrate the Cyber Safety Helpdesk website.
  • Explain suspicious links and phishing.
  • Explain fake accounts and impersonation.
  • Provide online fraud prevention awareness.
  • Explain basic account-security practices.
  • Guide users about official cybercrime reporting.

🎯 Purpose

The purpose of Cyber Help Desk Day is to increase community awareness about cyber safety and help people understand basic steps to protect their personal information and online accounts.

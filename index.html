🔐 Basic Cyber Safety Tips

Follow these simple steps to stay safer online.

🔑

Strong Password

Use strong and unique passwords for different online accounts.

📲

OTP Safety

Never share OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords with unknown people.

🔗

Check Links

Do not click suspicious links received through unknown messages or emails.

💳

Payment Safety

Always verify the receiver before making an online payment.

🔐

Use 2FA

Enable Two-Factor Authentication whenever it is available.

👤

Fake Profiles

Verify social-media profiles before sharing information or sending money.

💻

Update Devices

Keep your operating system and apps updated with security fixes.

📢

Report Scams

Report cyber incidents through official government channels.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is every unknown number a scam?

No. An unknown number is not automatically a scam. Verify the caller independently before taking action.

Should I share my OTP with someone?

No. Never share OTPs with callers or unknown people.

What should I do after financial cyber fraud?

Contact your bank through its official channel and report financial cyber fraud through the appropriate official channels, including 1930 in India.

Can this website guarantee that a number is safe?

No. The checker only displays information available in its database. It cannot guarantee that a number is safe or genuine.

🔐 Cyber Safety Tips

  • Never share OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN or passwords.
  • Do not open unknown or suspicious links.
  • Verify a caller before making any payment.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
  • If a message creates urgency or threatens account closure, verify it through the organisation's official channel.
  • Keep your phone, browser and apps updated.
